:::story Ursula’s rule
Ursula is 80 and lives in Bern. After her husband died, she made one rule with her niece Clara: “Before I send money to anyone new, I tell you first.”

Months later, a charming online friend needs CHF 2,000 “for customs”. He writes: “Please don’t tell your family, they won’t understand.” Ursula remembers her rule. She calls Clara and reads her the messages. Clara is worried. Ursula waits a day, and the friend becomes angry and pushy. That tells her everything.

*Ursula and Clara are made up.*
:::end

:::remember
**Before money moves: tell one person you trust, show them the messages, and if they are worried, stop.**
:::end

## Why the test works

Scams work best in secret and in a hurry. A second person, outside the story, can see warning signs that are hard to see from inside.

The FTC’s first advice for anyone who suspects a romance scam is to stop talking to the person and to talk to someone you trust, asking whether friends or family are worried about the new love interest ([FTC: Romance scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams)). For investments, it advises taking time to talk to someone you trust, someone you know has your best interests in mind ([FTC: Investment scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/investment-scams)).

The Swiss Federal Office for Cyber Security adds that someone who really loves or values you won’t put you under emotional pressure. Refuse money demands even if the person threatens to end contact ([BACS: Woche 32](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/25w32-de)).

## The test in four steps

1. **Pause.** Someone you know only online, or a caller, wants you to send, move or invest money.
2. **Tell one trusted person.** A family member, a friend, your doctor, your bank.
3. **Show them.** Read out the messages or show them the screen.
4. **Listen.** If they are worried, stop. If the request is real, it can wait a day.

## Warning signs during the test

- **“Don’t tell anyone.”** A request for secrecy protects the scammer, not you.
- **“It must be today.”** Pressure is one of the clearest signs (lesson 00.02).
- **Anger when you hesitate.** A real friend understands caution.

:::practice scenes scene=trusted

:::exercise Choose your trusted person
Write down the name and number of one person you would call before sending money to someone new. Then tell them: “If I ever call you about sending money, ask me lots of questions.”
:::solution
Good choices are people who are calm, who don’t judge, and who are easy to reach. Add their number to your trusted-numbers card (lesson 00.04). If you live alone, your bank or a friend from a club can be your trusted person too.
:::end

:::helper
Offer to be someone’s trusted person. Agree on it out loud and keep it light: “Before any money goes to someone new, call me, no matter what. I promise I won’t be cross.” Lesson 11.04 covers this in more detail.
:::end
