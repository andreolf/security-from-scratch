Her friend Paul did it differently: pencil, paper, an envelope in a drawer at home. Nobody but him knows where.

*Nadia and Paul are made up.*

**Your secret phrase is the whole safe. Paper only, hidden at home. Never photograph it, type it into a website, or tell anyone.**

## What the phrase is

When you create a wallet, it shows you a list of words, usually 12 or 24. ethereum.org explains that this recovery phrase (also called the secret recovery phrase or seed phrase) is the master key to your wallet: anyone who has it can access all your accounts and drain every asset ([ethereum.org: Security](https://ethereum.org/en/security/)).

With these words, the wallet can be rebuilt on any device, anywhere. That helps you if you lose your phone, and it helps a thief just as much.

## How to keep it

- **Write it on paper** and check each word carefully.
- **Keep it hidden at home**, somewhere safe from fire and water if you can.
- **No photo or screenshot.** ethereum.org warns that screenshots can sync to the cloud, where hackers look for them ([ethereum.org](https://ethereum.org/en/security/)).
- **Never type it into a website**, a form, or a chat.
- **Never tell anyone**, not family online, not “support”, not a friend who offers to help.

If you lose the phrase and the device, nobody can recover the wallet for you. The FTC notes that no one can step in to recover funds from a lost or stolen wallet ([FTC: Cryptocurrency and scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-cryptocurrency-scams)).

:::helper
If you help a relative set up a wallet, look away when the words appear. Let them write them down. You don’t need to know them, and it is safer if you don’t.
:::end
