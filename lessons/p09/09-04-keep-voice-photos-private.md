:::story Pia’s birthday video
Pia is 69 and lives in Chur. For her birthday, her grandson posts a lovely video of her speaking to the camera on his public Instagram. Everyone can see it.

Later, at a talk at her senior centre, Pia hears that a few seconds of voice can be enough to copy it. She asks her grandson to make his account private, and they set her WhatsApp profile photo to “My contacts”. “It’s still a lovely video,” she says. “Now it’s just for family.”

*Pia is made up.*
:::end

:::remember
**Share your voice and face with people you know, not with everyone. Make accounts private and set your profile photo to “My contacts”.**
:::end

## Where scammers get voices and faces

The Swiss Federal Office for Cyber Security (BACS) explains that criminals get voice samples from social media and other public sources, and usually find their victims’ contact details in the public phone book ([BACS: Tips for seniors](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/ecsm24-4-de)). It adds that in the age of social media, the supply of video material for deepfakes is almost endless ([BACS: Woche 31](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26w31-de)).

The FBI advises: if possible, limit online content of your image or voice, make social media accounts private, and limit followers to people you know ([FBI IC3: Generative AI PSA](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA241203)).

:::figure share-voice
Leave less for scammers to copy.
:::end

## Your profile photo on WhatsApp

WhatsApp says that by default, any user can see your profile photo. You can change that so only your contacts see it ([WhatsApp Help Center: Privacy settings](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3307102709559968/?cms_platform=iphone&locale=en_US)).

:::try Show your WhatsApp photo to contacts only
--- WhatsApp on iPhone
1. Open WhatsApp and go to **Settings**, then **Privacy**.
2. Tap **Profile photo**.
3. Choose **My contacts**.

Source: [WhatsApp Help Center: How to change your privacy settings](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3307102709559968/?cms_platform=iphone&locale=en_US)
--- WhatsApp on Android
1. Open WhatsApp and tap **More options** (the three dots), then **Settings**, then **Privacy**.
2. Tap **Profile photo**.
3. Choose **My contacts**.

Source: [WhatsApp Help Center: How to change your privacy settings](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3307102709559968/?cms_platform=android&locale=en_US)
:::end

You can do the same for your “About” information and when you were last online ([WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3307102709559968/?cms_platform=iphone&locale=en_US)).

## Beyond WhatsApp

- **Make social media accounts private**, and accept only people you know ([FBI IC3](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA241203)).
- **Think before posting videos where you talk**, especially public ones.
- **Ask family to check too.** Videos of you on their public accounts count as well.
- **Share less personal detail**, like holidays or family names, which scammers can use to make their story believable (lesson 06.04).

This isn’t about hiding from the world. Lesson 09.03’s code word protects you even if your voice is public. Sharing less just makes the scammer’s job harder.

:::exercise Your public footprint
Search for your own name in a search engine and on Facebook or Instagram, while logged out if you can. What can a stranger see: photos, videos, your town, your family’s names?
:::solution
Make a list of what you’d rather keep private. Change the settings you can, and ask family to make posts about you visible to friends only. If something stays public, that’s fine. It is one more reason to always call back and use your code word.
:::end

:::helper
Do the profile photo steps together with your relative. Then check your own accounts: are there public videos of them, with their voice, that you could make private?
:::end
