:::story Tea and a checklist
Sophie is 38 and lives in Lausanne. She visits her aunt Margrit, 82, with a cake and this page printed out. They take their time. Margrit’s phone has updates waiting; they turn on automatic updates. Her email has no two-step sign-in; they turn it on. They fill in the trusted-numbers card and choose a family code word.

When they finish, Margrit says: “That wasn’t so bad.” They agree to do it again in spring.

*Sophie and Margrit are made up.*
:::end

:::end

## The four things

The title says 30 minutes, but take as long as you need, and split it over several days if that is easier. There is no timer.

:::figure safety-afternoon
A safety afternoon, in four steps.
:::end

1. **Turn on automatic updates** on the phone, tablet and computer. The Swiss Federal Office for Cyber Security (BACS) explains that criminals exploit security holes in software, and advises turning on automatic updates for the system and all apps ([BACS: S-U-P-E-R.ch updates](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/ks24-1-en)). Steps: lesson 05.02.
2. **Turn on two-step sign-in** for the main email account. The US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) advises turning on multifactor authentication, starting with email ([CISA: Turn on MFA](https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/turn-mfa)). Steps: lesson 04.03.
3. **Fill in the trusted-numbers card:** the bank’s block number from the back of the card, the police, and family (lesson 00.04).
4. **Agree a family code word**, said out loud, never written in a message (lesson 09.03).

## If you have more time

- Set up a password manager together (lesson 03.04).
- Check privacy settings on WhatsApp and social media (lessons 06.06 and 09.04).
- Talk through the “call me first” rule (lesson 11.04).

:::exercise Your safety afternoon checklist
Print this page or write the four things on paper. Tick each one as you do it together: updates, two-step sign-in, trusted-numbers card, code word. Write the date at the top.
:::solution
If one step doesn’t work, for example you can’t find a setting, skip it and note it. Come back to it next time. Three out of four is already a big improvement. Agree a date to check again, for example in six months.
:::end

:::helper
Bring something nice, keep it relaxed, and let your relative lead. If they get tired, stop and continue another day.
:::end
