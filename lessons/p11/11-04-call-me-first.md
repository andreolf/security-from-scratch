:::story Ilse’s rule
Ilse is 79 and lives in Lucerne. Her neighbour Tom, 52, made a deal with her over coffee: “Before you pay anyone you don’t know, or share any code, call me first. Any time. I won’t be cross.”

Three weeks later, “her grandson” texts from a new number asking for CHF 1,800 and says not to tell his parents. Ilse calls Tom. Together they call her grandson’s old number. He knows nothing about it.

*Ilse and Tom are made up.*
:::end

:::remember
**Before you pay, share a code, or keep a secret: call your “call me first” person.**
:::end

## Why it works

Scams depend on urgency and secrecy. The US Federal Trade Commission describes how fake-emergency scammers say it’s urgent, that you’re the only one who can help, and that you must keep it secret. Its advice: hang up or say you’ll call back, call the person on a number you know, and call someone else in the family, even if you were told to keep it secret ([FTC: Fake emergencies](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/scammers-use-fake-emergencies-steal-your-money)).

A “call me first” person makes that easy. The pause breaks the pressure, and a second person sees what the scammer hid. For romance scams, the FTC also advises talking to someone you trust ([FTC: Romance scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams)).

## How to set it up

1. **Choose the person** together: someone calm, reachable, and who won’t blame.
2. **Agree when to call:** before paying anyone new, buying gift cards or crypto, sharing a code, letting someone onto the computer, or keeping something secret from the family.
3. **Agree there’s no shame:** calling for nothing is a success, not a bother.
4. **Have a backup person** for when the first one is away.
5. **Put both numbers** on the trusted-numbers card and in Contacts (lesson 00.04).

## When you are the person

Answer warmly. Thank them for calling. Check together, calmly, on numbers you find yourself. If it is a scam, help block and report it (lesson 10.03). If it was real, the money can wait a few minutes.

:::helper
Say it out loud more than once: “You can call me about anything, any time. I will never be cross.” People only call if they are sure there is no blame.
:::end
