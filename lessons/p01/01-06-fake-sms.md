:::story The parcel that never came
Heidi is 74 and loves ordering seeds online. One evening a text arrives: “Your parcel could not be delivered. Pay CHF 1.50 to reschedule.” There is a link.

Heidi is expecting seeds, so it makes sense. But she remembers: don’t tap links in unexpected messages. She opens the delivery company’s app herself. There is no parcel waiting, and no fee to pay. She deletes the text.
:::end

:::remember
**Don’t tap links in text messages you didn’t expect. Open the app or website yourself instead.**
:::end

## What fake text messages look like

Fake texts are a kind of phishing, sometimes called [[term:smishing]]. They try to make you tap a link, call a number or share details. The US Federal Trade Commission lists common examples: fake package delivery notices, messages about “suspicious activity” on your account, a problem with your payment, a fake invoice, or a free prize ([FTC: Spam text messages](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)).

:::figure sms-anatomy
The parts of a fake text message, and what each part tries to do.
:::end

## Warning signs

- You **didn’t expect** it: a parcel fee, a refund, a prize, a blocked account.
- It **hurries** you: “today”, “within 24 hours”, “last reminder”.
- It wants you to **tap a link** or **reply with details or a code**.
- The link’s website name is **not the company’s own address** (lesson 02.02 shows how to read it).

The FTC is clear: legitimate companies won’t ask for information about your account by text ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)). And as lesson 01.05 explains, a fake text can even appear in the same conversation as real messages from that company ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/phishing-de)).

## A new trick in Switzerland

Since summer 2025, criminals in Switzerland have also used so-called “SMS blasters” ([BACS half-year report](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/hjb-25-2-de)). The report explains that they let criminals get around the filters that phone companies use against fake texts. You don’t need to understand the technology. The rule stays the same: don’t tap the link.

## What to do

1. **Don’t tap the link**, and don’t reply.
2. **If you think it might be real**, open the company’s app or website yourself, or call a number you already have ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)).
3. **Report it and delete it.** In the US you can forward spam texts to 7726 ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-report-spam-text-messages)). In Switzerland, you can report phishing on [antiphishing.ch](https://www.antiphishing.ch/), which is run by the Swiss Federal Office for Cyber Security ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/phishing-de)).

:::try Report a fake text and delete it
Reporting helps your phone and your phone company stop similar messages.

--- iPhone
1. Open the **Messages** app.
2. If you haven’t opened the message: swipe left on it, tap the delete button, then tap **Delete and Report Spam**.
3. If you have opened it: tap **Report Spam** at the bottom of the message (it appears for unknown senders), then tap **Delete and Report Spam**.

You can’t report a message after you have replied to it. Checked against: [Apple Support: Report spam and block senders in Messages](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/report-spam-and-block-senders-iph3f94d910d/ios).

--- Android
1. Open the **Messages** app (Google Messages).
2. Touch and hold the conversation.
3. Tap **Block**, then **Report spam**, then **OK**.

Other messaging apps may use different words. Checked against: [Google Messages Help: Report spam & block numbers](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9061432).
:::end

:::helper
Many people worry they have to answer or “unsubscribe” from a fake text. Explain that they don’t: delete and forget is enough. Practise the report steps together on a real spam message if one arrives. If your relative tapped a link and typed in details, help them call their bank straight away.
:::end
